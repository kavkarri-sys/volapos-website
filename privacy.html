Section 7

International transfers

Some of the processors listed in Section 6 are based outside the United Kingdom, principally in the United States. When personal data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on one or more of the following safeguards as required by UK GDPR Chapter V:

  • a UK adequacy regulation in force at the time of transfer (for example, the UK,US Data Bridge for certified US recipients);
  • the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses; or
  • any other lawful transfer mechanism recognised under UK GDPR.

We carry out a transfer risk assessment before relying on a non-adequacy mechanism. A copy of the relevant safeguards is available on request, see Section 14 for how to contact us.

Section 9

How we protect it

We have implemented technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS) for all data flowing between your device and our website and processors;
  • access controls and the principle of least privilege for staff and processors;
  • regular review of supplier security posture before onboarding and during the relationship;
  • secure development practices for the website and chatbot;
  • incident detection and response procedures, including notification to the ICO and affected individuals where required by law.

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Information Commissioner's Office without undue delay and, where the risk is high, we will also notify you.

Section 10

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Access , you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification , you can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") , you can ask us to delete your data where we no longer have a lawful basis to keep it.
  • Restriction , you can ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability , you can ask us to provide your data in a structured, machine-readable format and, where technically feasible, to transfer it to another controller.
  • Object , you can object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing. If you object to direct marketing, we will stop.
  • Automated decision-making , you have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not make such decisions.
  • Withdraw consent , where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 14. We will respond within one month of receiving a valid request. We may ask for proof of identity before responding to ensure we are giving the data to the right person.

Exercising your rights is free. If a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse to act, as permitted by Article 12(5).

Section 11

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies on volapos.com. Strictly necessary cookies are set without your consent because the site cannot function without them. All other cookies (analytics, marketing, embedded third-party content such as Google Maps) are set only with your consent, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 ("PECR").

A standalone Cookie Policy with the name and purpose of each cookie will be published alongside our cookie consent banner. Until that is live, the categories of cookies that may be set on this website are summarised below.

  • Strictly necessary, required for the site to function (e.g. CSRF protection on forms). Set without consent.
  • Analytics, Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-0D679KWTZ2), used to understand aggregate site usage. Personal identifiers are redacted before transmission.
  • Embedded third-party content, Google Fonts (typography) and, on the Contact page, Google Maps (location display). These may set cookies and transfer data to Google.

If you do not wish any of the above to be set, you can configure your browser to block cookies. We will publish a granular cookie consent banner shortly so you can manage each category individually.
