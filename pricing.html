The honest answers

Questions we hear most often from café and restaurant owners.

How much does VolaPos cost?

Three plans: Vola Lite £29/till/mo for single-site cafés, Vola Plus £69/till/mo for growing venues, and Vola Pro custom-quoted for multi-site restaurants and takeaways (depends on sites, modules and complexity). Hardware is quoted separately based on your setup.

Can I upgrade from Lite to Plus or Pro later?

Yes, upgrading is a single click. Your menu, staff, customers and reports carry over. No migration project, no downtime. You only pay the difference from the day you upgrade.

What hardware do I need?

For Lite we recommend the Sunmi dual-screen countertop unit (~£700) with built-in printer and cash-drawer kicker, plus a standard cash drawer (~£55) and your card payment terminal. Plus and Pro hardware is quoted to your setup, handhelds, kitchen displays, kiosks, label printers.

Does VolaPos integrate with card terminals?

Yes, with most leading UK card payment terminals. Send the amount from the till, the terminal handles the card flow, and the result returns automatically. Z reports reconcile cleanly against your card settlement. Not every terminal model is supported, we'll confirm compatibility with your current acquirer and hardware before you commit.

Is there a setup fee or long contract?

Yes, a one-off setup fee applies. It covers installation, configuration and staff training, and we'll quote the exact amount up front so you see the full cost before committing. Lite and Plus are month-to-month after setup; Pro is typically a 12-month term given the hardware and onboarding involved. We'll always be transparent about terms up front.
